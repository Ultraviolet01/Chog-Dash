    2 · SURVIVE
    • Dodge asteroids and space debris. One hit ends the run.
    • Speed keeps rising the further you fly. There is no boost.
    • Stardust: every 100 gives an extra life (up to 3). A life saves you from one crash.
    • Near misses flash on screen when you scrape past.
    3 · PICKUPS

    Pickups start after 10 seconds. Grab a power-up that is already running and it lasts longer.

    4 · ZONES

    Fly through the warp gate into each new zone. After the Milky Way the loop starts again, and every zone lasts longer than the last.

    5 · CHOG NFT OR PRACTICE
    6 · QUESTS & STREAK · CHOG HOLDERS
    • Quests: 3 dailies (easy, medium, hard) and 1 weekly, in Chog NFT runs. Each one earns a badge.
    • A streak day needs both: all 3 dailies done and 5 minutes flown in Chog runs that day. Days change at 00:00 UTC.
    • Keep it going every day. Miss a full day and it starts again from day 1. Your best streak is kept.
    • Streak lives: every 7 days in a row banks lives: 7 days +1, 14 days +3, 21 days +5, and so on. They are earned again after a restart.
    • Streak lives are used after your Stardust lives, in Chog NFT runs only. Unused ones stay banked.
