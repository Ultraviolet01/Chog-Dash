Ride a rocket through space and dodge asteroids and debris for as long as you can.

PRACTICE MODE

Play as Rookie

  • Rookie is the practice pilot, not a Chog NFT.
  • No wallet needed.
  • Your best distance is saved on this device only.
  • Practice runs never go on the NFT leaderboard.
CHOG NFT MODE

Ride your Chog

  • Connect a wallet that owns a Chog Genesis NFT on Monad.
  • Ride the rocket as the Chog you pick.
  • Abilities and tier multiplier come from its traits.
  • Validated runs go on the official NFT leaderboard.
Controls: move the mouse and the rocket follows it, up, down, left and right. Or use the keyboard: W/A/S/D or the arrow keys move it, and it stays put when you let go. Click or Space starts a run. E to fire Laser Eyes (only for Chogs with that trait).
On touch screens: drag to fly the rocket in any direction, and use the on-screen LASER button (Laser Eyes Chogs only).
