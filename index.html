How to play

Controls

  • Mouse: the rocket follows it, up, down, left and right.
  • Keyboard: W/A/S/D or the arrow keys. The rocket stays put when you let go.
  • Touch: drag to fly in any direction.
  • Start: click, tap or press Space.
  • Full screen: press F or the full-screen button. Esc or F again to leave.
  • Laser Eyes: E or the LASER button (Chogs with that trait only).

Zones

  • Outer Space 2 min
  • Asteroid Belt 4 min
  • Galactic Drift 8 min
  • Deep Cosmic Zone 14 min
  • Milky Way 22 min, then the loop starts again, each zone longer than the last.

Pickups

  • Stardust: every 100 gives an extra life (hold up to 3). A crash with a life continues the run.
  • Golden Horn: rare, worth 50 Stardust.
  • Dash: speed burst that smashes through anything for 3 s.
  • Magnet: pulls in Stardust and Horns for 10 s.
  • Slow-Mo: slows everything for 6 s.
  • Nova: clears every small obstacle on screen.
  • Radar: outlines obstacles for 12 s (Deep Cosmic Zone only).
  • Mystery Box: a random power-up.
  • Grabbing a power-up that is already running adds its time.

Modes

  • Practice: fly as Rookie, the practice pilot. No wallet. Best distance saved on this device only, never ranked.
  • Chog NFT: ride a Chog Genesis NFT you own. Abilities and tier multiplier come from its traits. Validated runs go on the leaderboard.
